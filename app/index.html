Data & credits

Built on NASA data, honest about its limits

NASA datasets

  • POWER daily rainfall and temperature, 1981 to present
  • MODIS MOD13Q1 NDVI via AppEEARS, for bare-field windows
  • SMAP L4 root-zone soil moisture, from 2015
  • GPM IMERG extreme-rain days and rainfall cross-check

Other data

  • SoilGrids (ISRIC, not NASA) pH, texture and organic carbon with 5th to 95th percentile bands

Limits

  • Results describe the surrounding area, not one field
  • Crop rules are rules of thumb, not measurements
  • Irrigation, pests and prices are not in the data

Not a replacement

This tool shows its working so you can discuss a change with a local agronomist. It does not replace one.
